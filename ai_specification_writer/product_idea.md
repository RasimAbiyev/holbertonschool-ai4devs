# Product Idea: ArtBazaar

## 1. Vision Statement

ArtBazaar is an online marketplace that connects artists, independent creators, and customers in Azerbaijan. Its goal is to make original artwork and handmade products more accessible while helping local creators showcase and sell their work through a dedicated digital platform.

Unlike NFT marketplaces, ArtBazaar focuses on physical original artworks and handmade products.

## 2. Primary Users and Their Needs

### Artists and Creators
- Need a platform to showcase and sell original artwork and handmade products.
- Want to manage product listings, descriptions, images, and prices.
- Need access to customers interested in local art and creative products.
- Want to maintain control over their products and pricing.

### Customers
- Want to discover and purchase original artwork and handmade products.
- Need clear product descriptions, images, and prices.
- Want a convenient way to contact sellers and place orders.
- Need a trustworthy shopping experience.

### Platform Administrator
- Needs to manage users, products, and marketplace content.
- Needs to review reported listings and address platform issues.
- Needs to monitor orders and maintain marketplace quality.

## 3. Key Features

1. **Artist and Seller Profiles:** Dedicated profiles where creators can present their work.
2. **Product Listings:** Product images, descriptions, categories, and prices.
3. **Product Discovery:** Search and category-based browsing.
4. **Marketplace:** A centralized platform for original artwork and handmade products.
5. **Order Management:** Tools for managing customer orders and purchase requests.
6. **AI-Powered Discovery (Planned):** AI-assisted product search and personalized recommendations.
7. **Administration:** User, product, and content management.

## 4. Key Differentiators

- Focus on physical original artwork and handmade products rather than NFTs.
- Dedicated marketplace for Azerbaijani artists and independent creators.
- No mandatory artist listing fees or commissions in the current business model.
- Potential integration of AI-powered search and recommendation features.
- Emphasis on supporting the local creative economy.

## 5. Product Scope

The initial product focuses on an online marketplace with seller profiles, product listings, product discovery, and order management.

Advanced AI recommendations and search capabilities are planned enhancements and are not represented as fully implemented features.
