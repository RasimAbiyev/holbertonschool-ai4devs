# ArtBazaar — User Stories

## User Story 1: Artist Registration

As an artist, I want to create an account so that I can showcase and sell my artwork on ArtBazaar.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Users can register with the required account information.
- The system validates required fields and prevents duplicate accounts.
- Successful registration provides access to an artist profile.

**Priority:** MVP

## User Story 2: Artist Profile Management

As an artist, I want to manage my profile so that customers can learn about me and my work.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Artists can add and edit their name, biography, and profile information.
- Artists can upload or update a profile image.
- Updated profile information is displayed on their public profile.

**Priority:** MVP

## User Story 3: Product Listing Creation

As an artist, I want to publish artwork listings so that potential customers can discover and purchase my products.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Artists can create listings with a title, description, price, category, and images.
- The system validates required product information.
- Published listings become visible in the marketplace.

**Priority:** MVP

## User Story 4: Product Listing Management

As an artist, I want to edit or remove my listings so that I can keep my product information accurate and up to date.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Artists can edit their own product descriptions, prices, and images.
- Artists can remove or deactivate their own listings.
- Artists cannot modify listings belonging to other users.

**Priority:** MVP

## User Story 5: Product Discovery

As a customer, I want to browse artwork and handmade products by category so that I can easily find items that interest me.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Customers can browse available product categories.
- The system displays relevant products within each category.
- Customers can open a product listing to view its details.

**Priority:** MVP

## User Story 6: Product Search

As a customer, I want to search for products by keywords so that I can quickly find specific artwork or handmade items.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Customers can enter keywords into a search field.
- Search results display products matching the entered keywords.
- The system displays an appropriate message when no products match the search.

**Priority:** MVP

## User Story 7: Seller Contact

As a customer, I want to contact an artist about a product so that I can ask questions before making a purchase.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Customers can access the seller's available contact option from a product listing.
- The system identifies the relevant seller and product.
- Customers can initiate a product-related inquiry.

**Priority:** High

## User Story 8: Order Management

As an artist, I want to view and manage customer orders so that I can track purchase requests and respond to customers.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Artists can view orders associated with their products.
- Each order displays relevant product and customer information.
- Artists can update an order's status.

**Priority:** MVP

## User Story 9: Content Moderation

As a platform administrator, I want to review and manage product listings so that inappropriate or misleading content can be addressed.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Administrators can view published product listings.
- Administrators can deactivate listings that violate platform rules.
- Administrative actions are restricted to authorized administrator accounts.

**Priority:** High

## User Story 10: AI-Powered Product Recommendations

As a customer, I want to receive personalized product recommendations so that I can discover artwork and handmade products relevant to my interests.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The system can generate recommendations based on available product information and customer preferences.
- Recommended products link to their corresponding product pages.
- The marketplace remains usable when personalized recommendations are unavailable.

**Priority:** Low — Planned Feature

## Summary

This document defines 10 user stories for the ArtBazaar marketplace, covering artists, customers, and platform administrators.

The MVP priorities focus on account management, artist profiles, product listings, product discovery, search, and order management. Advanced AI-powered recommendations are planned for a future development phase.
